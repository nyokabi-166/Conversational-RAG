# Figure Captions and Alt Texts (final Round 5 pack)

All figures are generated by `analysis_extras/final/make_figures.py` from the
final 2026-11-13 evaluation tables and the 2026-11-17 human-review labels. Each
figure exists as a 300-dpi PNG and a vector SVG in `analysis_extras/final/`.
Files below supersede the older `fig1_...`�?"`fig8_...` specifications; those
drafts used pilot/Round 4 numbers and must not be reported as final results.

Common provenance: `backend/evaluation/results/evaluation_report.csv`
(201 questions; 195 answerable, 6 unanswerable), 157-chunk rebuilt index from
12 official Kenya Airways sources, grounded Round 5 prompt, temperature 0.0,
seed 42, generation `meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-Turbo` (DeepInfra),
judge `google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (DeepInfra), RAGAS 0.4.3.

- **Files**: `fig_ragas_overall.png`, `fig_ragas_overall.svg`
- **Data**: `analysis_extras/final/overall_metrics.csv`

### Figure 2: RAG versus No-Retrieval Baseline

- **Files**: `fig_rag_vs_baseline.png`, `fig_rag_vs_baseline.svg`
- **Data**: `analysis_extras/final/overall_metrics.csv`,
  `analysis_extras/final/paired_tests.csv`
- **Caption**: **Figure 2: RAG versus no-retrieval baseline on the two shared
  RAGAS metrics ($n=201$).** RAG improved answer correctness from 0.235 to
  0.618 (+0.383, 95% bootstrap CI [+0.351, +0.414], paired t and Wilcoxon
  $p<0.001$) but scored lower on answer relevancy (0.706 versus 0.778;
  difference −0.072, 95% CI [−0.102, −0.044]).
- **Alt text**: Grouped bar chart comparing RAG and baseline answer relevancy
  and answer correctness, with score labels.

### Figure 7: Human versus RAGAS Answer Correctness

- **Files**: `fig_human_judge_agreement.png`,
  `fig_human_judge_agreement.svg`
- **Data**: `analysis_extras/validation_sample.csv`,
  `analysis_extras/final/agreement_results.json`
- **Caption**: **Figure 7: Agreement between human correctness labels and
  thresholded RAGAS scores on the 44 answerable validation questions.**
  At a RAGAS threshold of 0.5, agreement was **84.1%** (kappa 0.61);
  at 0.7, agreement was **72.7%** (kappa 0.45). The six unanswerable questions
  are excluded from this calculation and reported separately in
  **Table 5.12** (unanswerable behaviour). The sample is purposive rather than a
  simple random sample, so the estimate is descriptive. At 0.7 most
  disagreements were answers the author labelled correct that RAGAS scored below
  threshold, so the judge is stricter than the author at that threshold. This is
  human-versus-judge agreement, not inter-human reliability.
- **Alt text**: Two 2-by-2 agreement matrices comparing human correctness
  labels with RAGAS pass/fail at thresholds 0.5 and 0.7.
